Inbox. Approvals and reports come first. "Check-in logged" shows the automatic contact from a conversation.

How it fits the decisions

  1. Four team chats are pinned: Announcements (drafts need approval; leaders skip it), Main chat, Daily wins and High signal. Anyone on the team can start a side group and becomes its admin.
  2. Tabs: Home · Chats · + · My business · More. My business holds Team (mindmap), People, Customers, Events, Wallet and Campaigns as live tiles; More keeps Reports, settings and team admin. The desktop sidebar uses the same groups.
  3. Only apprentices, IBOs and leaders use chat. Prospects and candidates don't see the Chats tab.
  4. Direct messages go to anyone you share a chat with. Report and block are in every message's menu; reports notify the leader.
  5. Posts write data. Event cards record Going, Maybe and Bought ticket; win cards are the logged sale, contact or ticket; a conversation in both directions logs a check-in for both people.
  6. Links to older posts and material show as a quote or a playable card, as in Jamal's reply and Anita's shared talk.
  7. Message history is kept forever by default; each chat can be set to auto-delete.
