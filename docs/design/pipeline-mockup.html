My business. Pipeline becomes the first tile: this month's MG1s against the goal, and a mini bar of where everyone sits.

How it works

  1. Two different numbers. A column's count is who is in that stage right now. "62 held this month" counts MG1s that actually happened this month, which is the activity number the 25% pace is measured on.
  2. Nobody counts by hand. Logging "Booked a meeting" or "Showed the plan" from the + moves the card; dragging or swiping a card records the activity with today's date.
  3. Leaders see the whole team (their downline's prospects, with whose prospect it is). Everyone else sees their own team's pipeline. Same visibility rules as People.
  4. Conversions show where people fall off. Each step's rate is compared with a team target (the targets here are examples to set together); the biggest gap gets a plain-language tip, and the hotspot grid shows which team member needs help at which step.
  5. Stale cards are flagged (no movement or contact in 14+ days) so follow-ups don't slip.
  6. The goal is a team setting (80 MG1s a month for the 25% level after about 6 months), with the trend for the last six months.
  7. Registered means they became an apprentice; the card leaves the board after the month and the person continues on the business path.
