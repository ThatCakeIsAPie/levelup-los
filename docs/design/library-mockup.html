How it works

  1. Channels, not folders. Each channel is a short course: lessons in order, optionally grouped into sections, with "Continue" taking you to the next one.
  2. Lessons are audio, video or documents. Audio and video remember where you stopped; documents open in the app.
  3. Finishing a lesson logs it to your learning automatically, so it counts toward your streak and weekly goal.
  4. Who builds channels: team leaders and moderators for everyone; smaller leaders for their own downline (shown as "From Anita"). Channels can be limited, e.g. "for IBOs".
  5. Rights: every upload asks "I have permission to share this"; videos can start as unlisted YouTube or Vimeo links to keep costs down.
  6. Share to chat: any lesson becomes a playable card in a chat, like the talk Anita shared in High signal.
