What changed

  1. One "Today" list replaces the two "Due for contact" lists, where 59 people showed "Never". Items are ranked by urgency and each has a one-tap action.
  2. Learning becomes a streak and a weekly goal instead of a stats table. Logging moves to the Log button, which is available on every screen.
  3. The next event gets its own card with a countdown and ticket progress, because tickets are what leaders chase.
  4. Leaders see their team at a glance: size, stage mix in the stage colors, and who's learning.
  5. On phones: bottom tabs, a floating Log button, and the same cards stacked.

What changed

  1. Read first, edit when needed. Today's page is a form of 10 always-editable fields. It becomes a summary, and the fields move to a Details tab with an Edit button.
  2. Call, Text, Email and Check in sit at the top, where your thumb is on a phone.
  3. The business path shows where they are and when they become an IBO. The 90- and 30-day notices appear here, explained in context.
  4. The 10-column event table becomes one card per event, each with status chips and the next step as a button.
  5. Team and customers are summarized beside it, in the same stage colors used everywhere else.

Not covered yet: the People list, Mindmap, Events and the Log sheet. Those come after we agree on this direction.
