What's different

  1. Five things, no more: Home, Customers (with the customer pipeline and your list), Events, Learning and Wallet. Phone tabs: Home · Customers · + · Events · Me.
  2. One goal at a time: the next milestone with its real number, not the whole ladder.
  3. The + button logs a sale or uploads a receipt first; that's what moves Core 150.
  4. A quiet hint says what sponsoring someone unlocks, so growth feels like a reward.

Unlocking. Reaching a milestone that opens new parts of the app says so once, then they simply appear.

How it fits the plan

  1. New apprentices see Home, Customers (pipeline, your list, customers), Events (attending), Learning and Wallet.
  2. First sponsor unlocks the team view (mindmap), downline profiles, the recruiting pipeline and Reports. 3/10 unlocks event planning. Leaders and their deputies see everything.
  3. Every milestone is detected from the data: PV from logged sales and receipts (uplines can override a month), sponsors and team size from the tree.
  4. Customer pipeline milestones are fixed (Contact, First meeting, First sale, On Ditto); steps between them are flexible, like the recruiting pipeline.
  5. Chats, the library and campaigns stay switched off for now, except for the admin account.
