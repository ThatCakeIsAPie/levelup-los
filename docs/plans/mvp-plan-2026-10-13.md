# MVP plan

Status: from Lyle's partner review, 2026-10-13. Mockup ([`design/mvp-mockup.html`](../design/mvp-mockup.html)) approved the same day; the build is under way on `develop`.

## Scope

The first release for the partner's team has **three modules**:

1. **Customers** — your own customers, sales, receipts and follow-ups.
2. **IBO progression** — your list, the recruiting pipeline, and the growth path from new apprentice to 12/100.
3. **Event planning** — events by category, who's going, who wants to go but has no ticket, attendance, and follow-ups.

Already built for later phases, switched **off** for everyone except the admin account:

| Feature                                         | Phase |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| Chats (and the inbox parts that depend on chat) | 2     |
| Library                                         | 2     |
| Campaigns                                       | 3     |

**Wallet** (shared trip costs) stays on for everyone: a new person going to a conference still needs to track what they owe and are owed.

### Feature switches and the admin login

- Each feature can be switched on or off for the whole team.
- An **admin account** sees every feature regardless of the switches, plus debug tools. It's the "experimental branch" login for trying phase 2 and 3 features against real data before turning them on.

## Two pipelines

1. **Your list → customer pipeline → recruiting pipeline.** Everyone starts on the left of a pipeline and is worked to the right:
   - **Customer pipeline** (from day one): new apprentices focus on sales, so they work their list toward customers first (see [Customer pipeline](#customer-pipeline)).
   - **Recruiting pipeline** (unlocks at first sponsor): the board already built — milestones Contact made → First meeting → First plan → Registered, with leader steps. By the time it unlocks, people already know how a pipeline works from their customers.
2. **Growth path** — everyone's progress after registering:

   New apprentice → First sale → Core 150 → Core 300 → First sponsor → Bronze foundation → 3/10 → Bronze builder → 6/25 → 6/50 → 7,500 Platinum → Ruby → 12/100

   More milestones can be added between these later; the order of these stays fixed.

   | Milestone         | Reached when                                                                  |
   | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | First sale        | Your first logged sale                                                        |
   | Core 150          | 150 PV in a month                                                             |
   | Core 300          | 300 PV in a month                                                             |
   | First sponsor     | Your first personally sponsored person registers                              |
   | Bronze foundation | 3 legs at 150 PV or more each, and 600 group PV in total, in the same month   |
   | 3/10              | 3 personal sponsors and 10 people in total in your group (not counting you)   |
   | Bronze builder    | 3 legs at 300 PV or more each, and 2,500 group PV in total, in the same month |
   | 6/25              | 6 personal sponsors, 25 people in total                                       |
   | 6/50              | 6 personal sponsors, 50 people in total                                       |
   | 7,500 Platinum    | 7,500 group PV in a month                                                     |
   | Ruby              | 15,000 group PV in a month                                                    |
   | 12/100            | 12 personal sponsors, 100 people in total                                     |

   A leg's PV is the group PV of everyone in that frontline person's line. "In a month" means within one calendar month, matching Amway's monthly cycle. Milestones fall into three classes by how long they count once met:

   - **Permanent** (New apprentice, First sale, First sponsor): once reached, it stays reached.
   - **Headcount, live** (3/10, 6/25, 6/50, 12/100): counts only while the person currently meets it (current personal sponsors and current people in their group). Dropping below un-checks it, and it becomes something to earn again.
   - **Volume, held for the following month** (Core 150, Core 300, Bronze foundation, Bronze builder, 7,500 Platinum, Ruby): held on a day if its rule was met in the current calendar month or the previous one. Hitting Platinum in September keeps it through October; if October ends without hitting it again, it falls back on November 1 (unless met in November).

   History is kept for every milestone: the first date it was ever reached (and how long it took) and, for headcount and volume ones, the most recent time it was met. The path shows three states: held now (checked), earned before but not held now ("First hit Mar 2026 · earn it again") and never reached. The next milestone is the first one in path order that isn't held now; later ones that are held still show as held. When a volume milestone is held only through last month, the person (and their uplines) see a heads-up: "Ruby: hit 15,000 group PV again this month to keep it." Unlocks go by "ever reached": someone who drops below 3/10 keeps event planning.

### Profile views

- **Your own profile:** what you've achieved so far (with dates) and **your next milestone**, with what's needed to reach it. One clear goal, not the whole ladder.
- **A downline's profile:** their **full path to 7,500 Platinum and beyond** — where they are, when each milestone was reached, how long each took, and what's next.

### Where the numbers come from

- **PV** comes from logged sales. The fastest way to log one is to **upload the receipt** from the Amway app (PDF or screenshot): the app reads it, fills in the customer, products, PV and date, and you confirm before it's saved. Receipts themselves stay in the Amway app; LevelUp only reads them to log the sale. Monthly PV is the sum of logged sales.
- **Uplines can update a downline's PV** for a month (their Amway app shows the real figure); the upline's number replaces the logged total for that month, and the change is recorded.
- **Every milestone above is detected automatically** from the sponsor tree and monthly PV. Uplines can still mark one by hand (for example history from before the app), which is recorded: on a permanent milestone the mark counts as reached; on a headcount or volume milestone it records when they first hit it (history only; it counts now only while they meet it, and a month's PV set by an upline covers volume).

## Your list

A new tab next to the pipelines. Each entry: name, how you know them, phone/email (optional), notes, and a warm/hot rating. Names on the list feed the customer pipeline's "Not contacted yet" column, and later the recruiting pipeline; one person can be on both.

- New apprentices build their list with their upline, who **can view and update their downline's list** (same visibility as the rest of the downline's data).

## Customer pipeline

The same board style as the recruiting pipeline, sized for a new apprentice: everyone starts on the left and is worked to the right.

- **Starting column:** **Not contacted yet** (names from your list). People you meet in the marketplace go straight to **Contact** (a separate "New contacts" column was merged into Contact on 2026-10-14).
- **Four fixed milestones:** **Contact → First meeting → First sale → On Ditto** (the monthly product subscription). These can't be renamed or reordered, so customer numbers roll up across the team like the recruiting pipeline.
- **Steps between milestones** (leaders define them, downlines inherit, hide or add their own — same rules as the recruiting pipeline). Starting set: Sampled product, Sample box sale, Product meeting 2+. They matter less than the milestones and can sit anywhere between them.
- **Outcomes on the right:** **Not interested** and **Do not contact**. "Do not contact" hides the person from reminders everywhere.

A first sale moves the person into Customers (and logs the sale); reaching On Ditto marks them as a subscription customer.

## What each person sees (progressive disclosure)

| Stage                                    | Sees                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New apprentice** (until first sponsor) | Home (next milestone, learning, upcoming events), Customers and the customer pipeline, Your list, Learning, your own profile, Events (attending only), Wallet                |
| **After first sponsor**                  | + Team view (the mindmap), downline profiles and their growth paths, the recruiting pipeline, Reports                                                                        |
| **3/10 and up**                          | + Event planning (create and edit events, tickets and travel tracking, shared costs)                                                                                         |
| **Leaders**                              | Everything, team settings and targets                                                                                                                                        |
| **Leader's deputies**                    | A leader can name people on their team who see everything the leader sees and can edit on the leader's behalf (for when the leader is away). Every edit records who made it. |

The navigation, My business tiles and Home cards already check the person's role; these rules replace those checks with "what has this person unlocked".

## Events

Events are grouped by **event categories**, which each team keeps for itself (Jay's requests, 2026-10-16: "different teams try out different types of events"). Every team starts with these, the six types the app had before:

| Starting category    | In person | Online | Tickets | Travel | Customers welcome |
| -------------------- | --------- | ------ | ------- | ------ | ----------------- |
| Night Owls           | ✓         |        |         |        |                   |
| Work session         | ✓         |        |         |        |                   |
| Info session         | ✓         | ✓      |         |        | ✓                 |
| Training             | ✓         | ✓      |         |        | ✓                 |
| Monthly team meeting | ✓         | ✓      | ✓       |        | ✓                 |
| Conference           | ✓         | ✓      | ✓       | ✓      |                   |

- A category carries what used to come from the type, as plain yes/no settings: "Can people join online?", "Do people buy tickets?" (ticket tracking and reminders), "Is there travel?" (travel plans, shared costs, qualifications: the full event page), "Remind uplines when someone misses one?" (on for all six) and "Are customers welcome?" (Home suggests inviting your customers). Each also has a name, an icon and a color.
- Anyone who plans events can add a category from the event form ("+ New category"). Leaders and their deputies tidy the list up in Team settings: rename, change the icon, color and settings, reorder, archive (old events keep it; it can't be picked for new ones; it can be brought back) and merge one into another (its events move over and it's archived).
- The Events list filters by the team's categories.
- Leaders and their deputies add and update events (and anyone 3/10 and up plans their own).
- People log whether they attended (and in person or virtual); uplines can log attendance for their downline.
- Attendance counts toward each person's activity, alongside reading and audios. Customers on an event's list count too: they're part of the business.
- **Missed-event follow-up (per category, where its setting is on):** if someone misses an event of a category they attended last time, everyone above them sees a follow-up in their Today list. For a customer, that's the customer's owner and the owner's uplines. It disappears for all of them as soon as one upline logs a follow-up with that person — or leaves a note saying it was already handled (for example "talked at the gym, she was sick"), which the others can read.

## Look and feel (approved 2026-10-13)

- **The LevelUp Leadership colors replace the LTD palette everywhere:** deep green for buttons and links, the logo green for progress and highlights, charcoal for the sidebar, IBO chips and event cards, cool greys for neutrals, amber kept for wins. The wordmark uses a typewriter face; body text stays Plus Jakarta Sans. Hex values were matched by eye from the logo image; swap in the exact codes when the logo file arrives.
- **The growth path scrolls sideways, not down:** a row of dots, one per milestone, joined by a line (filled up to the current one). Tapping a dot shows that milestone's details (what it takes, when it was reached and how long it took, or progress toward it). The current milestone is selected when the path opens. Same layout on desktop and phone.

## Build order

1. **Wave 1 (two agents in parallel):** growth path (milestone engine, PV on sales, monthly PV overrides, hand-marked milestones, path UI, Home card) and Your list + customer pipeline. Rebrand alongside.
2. **Wave 2:** unlocks + feature switches + admin login + deputies (needs the milestone engine), and event types + missed-event follow-ups.
3. **Then:** QA in both data modes, a preview Lyle can click through, and receipt reading once the sample receipt arrives.

## Jay's requests (2026-10-16)

Sixteen UX requests from Jay, with Lyle's answers:

- **Team map structure** reads "frontline/total", like the milestones: "4/27" = 4 frontline, 27 people in the group.
- **Referral source:** a column next to Not interested on both pipelines, for people who aren't a fit but pass on names. People they refer show "Referred by …".
- **Event categories replace the six fixed types.** Teams try different kinds of events, so categories are team-specific and editable rather than a fixed list plus extras. The current six become the starting categories; each category carries what used to come from the type (in person / virtual, tickets, travel). Filtering uses the categories.
- **Customers at events** count toward attendance and missed-event follow-ups; they're part of the business too.
- **Today on Home** is grouped by who the item is about: **Team** (registered people only — a prospect never shows under Team), **Customers**, **Pipeline** (people in progress, split into the recruiting and customer pipelines) and **Events**.
- Also: draggable dashboard cards, a "what you did today" activity card, Log button top right on desktop, breadcrumbs, phone formatting, hot/warm/cold on new contacts, Delete in the ⋯ menu, "Create event" on a profile, every attendee field editable (incl. back to "Not asked"), attendee filter by apprentice/IBO/customer/prospect, include-prospects toggle on the team map, multi-select groups and bulk actions on People.
